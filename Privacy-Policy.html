Privacy Policy

QR Scanner2025 App (“the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our App.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Your privacy is our top priority. The App is designed to perform its core function—scanning QR codes—without collecting, storing, or transmitting any personal information or sensitive data from your device.

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • No Image Storage: The images taken by your camera for scanning are processed on your device and are not stored, saved, or uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers. The images are used only for the purpose of detecting the QR code in real-time.
  • No QR Code Data Storage: The data contained within a QR code, which is displayed on the next screen, is not stored or saved by the App once you navigate away from that screen. We do not keep a history of scanned QR codes.

2. Camera and Internet Usage

To function, the App requires access to your device’s camera and internet connection.

  • Camera Access: The camera is used solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes. The camera's live feed is processed on your device to detect QR codes, and no video or image data is recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • Internet Access: The App uses your internet connection only when you choose to open a URL found within a QR code. This action is initiated by your explicit choice and is not done automatically. The App does not track or store the URLs you visit.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that collect, monitor, or analyze your personal information.

4. Your Consent

By using the App, you consent to our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
